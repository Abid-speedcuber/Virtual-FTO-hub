How chords, modes, and depth work

A+F means hold A, then press F. A F means tap A, then F within 1.4 seconds. Use physical codes such as KeyF, ShiftLeft, or Numpad8 when needed. A trigger can have one action per mode; other modes may reuse it.

Defaults: 1–9 set the next turn’s width; 0 sets 10. Shift+1–9 set 11–19, and Shift+0 sets 20. Shift+8 (also written *) sets 18. Release Shift before your move for a wide turn. Keep or hold Shift for a single slice at that depth. Ctrl holds wide; Alt reverses direction. A depth applies to the next accepted turn only. Unbound keys, held action keys, and mode switches do not consume it. Press the suffix again or Escape to cancel it.

TPS counts accepted manual turns, including sticker drags and face buttons. Depth keys, mode switches, held actions, undo/redo, auto-repeat, and automatic scrambles do not count. Peak and total reset with Reset input stats; the window is a trailing rolling average.

Find your flow.

Swipe to turn (default): drag on a sticker along a row to turn that slice in the direction of your drag. Drag empty space to orbit. Two fingers rotate the view and pinch to zoom. Mouse wheel zooms; right-drag orbits. Slice gestures select their own slice. Use the Slice menu for button and keyboard turns. Each puzzle size saves its own solve.

Orbit + click (original mode): drag horizontally around the fixed viewer Y axis or vertically around the fixed viewer X axis, regardless of puzzle orientation. Both allow repeated full turns; combining them can produce roll. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Hold the puzzle and scroll to roll around Z in 3° steps (wheel up counterclockwise, down clockwise). Click a sticker to turn its face; right-click a sticker or face button for a counterclockwise turn. Use the direction toggle to reverse keyboard or left-click turns.

Keyboard defaults: U R F L B D turn their labels. J = BR, K = BL. Keyboard bindings are fully editable in Settings → Keyboard & bindings. Numbers set one-shot move depths; Shift holds slice, Ctrl holds wide, and Alt reverses. Mouse/button modifiers still use Shift for wide, Ctrl for inner slice, and Ctrl+Shift for ultra wide. These modifiers work with keyboard input, face buttons, and mouse clicks, and override the depth selector. The depth tabs follow held modifiers and restore your selected depth on release. Without modifiers, the depth selector applies.

Undo: Ctrl/⌘ Z or Backspace. Redo: Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z or Ctrl Y. Home returns the view; Escape cancels queued inputs.

Orientation: labels follow the upper and front faces in all 48 CIF/EIF holds. During a turn, labels stay fixed. Turn off “Follow the view” for fixed notation.

Scrambles are undoable. Your puzzle and history save automatically in this browser. Reset to solved restores the puzzle and clears history after confirmation, keeping your view.
