    Leaderboard rules

    Only personal bests from signed-in, online 3 × 3 solves are submitted. Every result needs administrator approval. An undo makes a single ineligible and counts as DNF in averages. Averages use consecutive attempts, dropping the fastest and slowest result. Signing out, losing connection or reloading starts a new sequence. Recordings of the top 10 approved singles are public.

    Pending submissions expire after 30 days. Temporary share links expire after 30 days; they are separate from leaderboard recordings.
