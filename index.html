    How chords, modes, and depth work

    A+F means hold A, then press F. A F means tap A, then F within 1.4 seconds. Use physical codes such as KeyF, ShiftLeft, or Numpad8 when needed. A trigger can have one action per mode; other modes may reuse it.

    Bindings and modes save separately for every puzzle size. On 3×3, Tab switches CIF/EIF-R, C switches CIF/EIF-L, and M switches CIF/E Center. F/J do U′/U. Numbers 2–9 select width; 0 selects 10 and 1 selects 11. Shift+1–9 select 11–19 and Shift+0 selects 20. On 3×3, Space also selects two-wide and Shift holds slice; Ctrl-wide and Alt-reverse start unbound. Add a next-move slice suffix to stack with a width without holding a key. Full-size widths are cube rotations, which can also be bound directly. A suffix applies to one accepted turn; unmapped keys and mode switches leave it waiting. Press a suffix again or Escape to clear it.

    TPS counts accepted manual turns, including sticker drags and face buttons. Depth keys, mode switches, held actions, undo/redo, auto-repeat, and automatic scrambles do not count. Peak and total reset with Reset input stats; the window is a trailing rolling average.

    Find your flow.

    Swipe to turn (default): drag on a sticker along a row to turn that slice in the direction of your drag. Drag empty space to orbit. Two fingers rotate the view and pinch to zoom. Mouse wheel zooms; right-drag orbits. Slice gestures select their own slice. Use the Slice menu for button and keyboard turns. Each puzzle size saves its own solve.

    Orbit + click (original mode): drag horizontally around the fixed viewer Y axis or vertically around the fixed viewer X axis, regardless of puzzle orientation. Both allow repeated full turns; combining them can produce roll. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Hold the puzzle and scroll to roll around Z in 3° steps (wheel up counterclockwise, down clockwise). Click a sticker to turn its face; right-click a sticker or face button for a counterclockwise turn. Use the direction toggle for left-click turns and keyboard bindings set to follow the solve controls.

    Keyboard defaults: F/J = U′/U, G/H = F′/F, E/D = L′/L, I/K = R/R′, R/U = B′/B, S/W = BL/BL′, L/O = BR′/BR, A/; = D/D′. Each size has independent editable bindings. On 3×3, Tab toggles EIF-R, C toggles EIF-L, and M toggles E Center; use the same key to return to CIF. Numbers set one-shot widths; an optional slice suffix stacks with width. Full-size widths rotate the view. Mouse/button modifiers use Shift for wide, Ctrl for slice, and Ctrl+Shift for three-wide.

    Undo: Ctrl/⌘ Z or Backspace. Redo: Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z or Ctrl Y. Home returns the view; Escape cancels queued inputs.

    Orientation: labels follow the upper and front faces in all 48 CIF/EIF holds. During a turn, labels stay fixed. Turn off “Follow the view” for fixed notation.

    Undo stops at the computer-generated scramble. Your puzzle and history save automatically in this browser. Reset to solved restores the puzzle and clears history after confirmation, keeping your view.
